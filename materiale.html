Our materials

Two finishes. Two stories.

Natural Mayan plaster

Chukum

Lime and resin from the Havardia albicans tree, native to the Yucatán Peninsula. Waterproof, breathable, antibacterial — and entirely natural.

Contemporary continuous finish

Béton Ciré

Modified cement applied in 2–3 mm layers over existing surfaces. Seamless continuity, mineral depth, more than 50 available shades.
